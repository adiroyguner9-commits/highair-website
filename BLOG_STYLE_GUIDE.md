# HighAir Blog Style Guide

This document defines the voice, structure, and rules for every blog post on highair-expeditions.com.
Follow it for every new post so all articles feel like they were written by the same person.

---

## 1. The Voice

HighAir writes like a guide who has been there. Knowledgeable, direct, human.
Not a travel agency brochure. Not a Wikipedia article. Something in between a good journalist and a friend who knows the mountains.

Three words to keep in mind when writing: **מדויק. אנושי. כבוד.**
Accurate. Human. Respectful of the mountains and of the reader.

---

## 2. Hebrew Language Rules

### 2.1 Level of language

Write in high, clear Hebrew. Not academic Hebrew, not street Hebrew.
The standard to aim for is a quality Israeli newspaper feature (Haaretz magazine, not a WhatsApp message).

Examples from our posts:

**Good:**
> "ההעפלה הראשונה של קאמי ריטה לפסגה התרחשה ב-1994, כשהיה בן 24. מאז, במשך שלושה עשורים, הוא חזר לפסגה כמעט מדי שנה."

> "אובדן כזה משאיר חותם. בתקופה שאחרי מות טנזין, הרילה התרחקה מהטיפוס."

**Avoid:**
> "קאמי ריטה הוא מאוד מדהים ועלה הרבה פעמים לאוורסט."

### 2.2 No dashes of any kind

**This is a hard rule. No exceptions.**

Do not use:
- Em dash: —
- En dash: –
- Hyphen as a pause: -

Replace every dash with a comma, a period, or rewrite the sentence.

**Wrong:**
> "הוא נשמעה עייפה מאוד — זה היה הדבר הראשון שדיווחה."

**Correct:**
> "היא נשמעה עייפה מאוד. זה היה הדבר הראשון שדיווחה."

**Wrong:**
> "האוורסט הוא הגבוה ביותר — 8,849 מטרים."

**Correct:**
> "האוורסט הוא הגבוה ביותר בעולם, 8,849 מטרים."

Hyphens are allowed only inside compound words or Hebrew date format: `ב-1985`, `כ-700`, `בן-אדם`.

### 2.3 Punctuation rules

- Sentences end with a period.
- Commas where a pause is needed, not a dash.
- Colon before a list or an explanation: `הסיבות ברורות: ראשית...`
- Question marks only in actual questions.
- No exclamation marks in body text. (They are acceptable in callout boxes, sparingly.)
- Numbers written as numerals: `32 פעמים`, not "שלושים ושתיים פעמים".
- Meters: `8,849 מ'` (with Hebrew apostrophe, not a straight quote).
- Dates: `ב-18 במאי 2026` (full format, not `18/5/26`).

### 2.4 Transliteration of foreign names

Write foreign names in Hebrew phonetically, consistently:
- Kristin Harila: קריסטין הרילה
- Tenzing Lama Sherpa: טנזין לאמה שרפה
- Kami Rita Sherpa: קאמי ריטה שרפה
- Denali: דנאלי
- Carstensz Pyramid: פירמידת קרסטנסז
- Kilimanjaro: קילימנג'רו (with geresh for the j sound)

Once you establish the Hebrew spelling in the first mention, use it consistently throughout the post.

## 3. English Language Rules

The English version of each post is a faithful translation, not a summary.
Match the structure block for block: same sections, same callouts, same images, same CTAs.

- Write in clear British-leaning English (not slang, not overly casual).
- No em dashes. Replace with commas or rewrite.
- Sentences should feel complete and self-contained.
- Do not use contractions in serious passages. "It is" not "it's" when the tone is formal.
- Numbers: numerals throughout (`32 times`, `8,849 m`).

## 4. Post Structure

Every post must follow this order. Do not skip sections.

```
1. Opening paragraphs (no section header)
2. Section: [main topic 1]
3. Section: [main topic 2]
   ...possibly with subheadings inside sections...
4. Section: [HighAir connection]
   CTA block
5. Section: שאלות נפוצות  ← exact wording, triggers FAQ schema
   heading + text pairs (5 questions minimum)
```

### 4.1 The opening (no section header)

The first paragraph is the most important sentence in the article.
Start with a specific moment: a time, a place, a person, a detail.
Never open with a definition or a general statement.

**Good opening (Harila post):**
> "בשעה מוקדמת של ה-17 במאי 2026, על פסגת נופטסה שגובהה 7,861 מטרים, נשמע ברדיו קולה של קריסטין הרילה. היא נשמעה עייפה מאוד."

**Good opening (Kami Rita post):**
> "ב-18 במאי 2026, בשעה 10:12 בבוקר שעון נפאל, עמד אדם אחד על פסגת האוורסט בפעם ה-32 בחייו."

**Good opening (Seven Summits post):**
> "ב-1985, ריצ'רד באס, עסקמן טקסאי בן 55, עלה לפסגת האוורסט ועמד על הנקודה הגבוהה ביותר בעולם."

**Bad opening:**
> "שבע הפסגות הן הפסגות הגבוהות ביותר בכל אחת משבע היבשות."

The second opening paragraph zooms out: why does this moment matter? Why should the reader care?
End the second paragraph with a bridge to the reader: connect the story to their own potential journey.

### 4.2 Sections

Section titles are short and conversational, not academic.
They read like subheadings in a magazine, not like chapter titles in a textbook.

**Good:**
- `מי זה קאמי ריטה שרפה?`
- `למה דווקא בלי חמצן`
- `הכל התחיל בהר אחד`
- `אז מאיפה מתחילים?`

**Avoid:**
- `רקע על הנושא`
- `סיכום ומסקנות`
- `מידע נוסף`

### 4.3 Subheadings

Use subheadings inside a section when the section covers more than two distinct items.
Each subheading is followed by a `text` block. Never leave a subheading without body text directly after it.

### 4.4 Callout boxes

Use one or two callout boxes per post, not more.
Reserve them for a single key fact, statistic, or concept that benefits from being separated visually.
Title starts with `✦ ` (sparkle + space).

**Good callout:**
```js
{ type: 'callout', title: '✦ המספרים מדברים', value: '11 מתוך 12 המטפסים שעלו לאוורסט הכי הרבה פעמים בהיסטוריה הם שרפות.' }
```

**Avoid:** Using a callout for a quote, an opinion, or anything that would read fine as regular body text.

### 4.5 CTAs

Every post has at least one CTA linking to a relevant HighAir expedition page.
Place it naturally, after a paragraph that makes the connection to the reader's own journey.
The CTA text is in Hebrew (the `text` field) and English (`textEn` field) on the same block.

Preferred CTA positions: after the "where to start" or "personal journey" section, and optionally a second CTA at the end before the FAQ.

### 4.6 FAQ section

Always end with the FAQ section. The section header must be exactly:
```js
{ type: 'section', value: 'שאלות נפוצות' }
```
(Hebrew posts) or
```js
{ type: 'section', value: 'Frequently asked questions' }
```
(English posts)

This exact wording triggers the automatic JSON-LD FAQ schema that Google reads.

Use `heading` + `text` pairs. Minimum 5 questions. Maximum 8.
Questions must be things a real person searching Google would type.
Answers are 2-4 sentences, complete and self-contained.

## 5. Images

### 5.1 Where to place images

- One image after the opening list or after the first major section.
- One image roughly in the middle of the post (after a records section, after a key revelation).
- One image just before the CTA or the final push toward the reader.
- Do not place two images back to back.

### 5.2 Real images vs. placeholders

If a real image is available in `/public/images/blog/`, use it:
```js
{ type: 'image', src: '/images/blog/filename.webp', alt: 'description', caption: 'Caption text' }
```

If no real image is available yet, use a colored gradient placeholder:
```js
{ type: 'image', grad: 'linear-gradient(135deg, #0f2240, #1e4a8a, #2d6b4f)', caption: 'Caption text' }
```
Do not leave an image block without a caption.

### 5.3 Caption style

Captions are short and factual. One sentence, no period at the end.
If there is a photo credit, add it at the end: `צילום: Name`

**Good:**
> `'האוורסט מהצד הדרומי, הנוף שקאמי ריטה מכיר טוב מכל אדם אחר על פני האדמה'`

**Good:**
> `'אוורסט ונופטסה, צילום: Alon Peleg'`

## 6. The HighAir Connection

Every post must mention HighAir naturally, not as an advertisement.
The mention comes out of the story itself: a connection between what the article describes and what HighAir does.

Standard patterns used in our posts:

**Pattern A: The first step**
> "ב-HighAir אנחנו מוציאים קבוצות ל[הר] כל שנה..."
Use when the post connects to an accessible expedition.

**Pattern B: The mission**
> "...חלק מהרווחים מכל משלחת שלנו מוקדש לתמיכה בחולי סרטן בישראל, כי ההרים, בעינינו, הם גם דרך לתת."
This sentence, or a version of it, appears in every post. It is part of who we are.

**Pattern C: The journey starts here**
> "כל מטפס שאתם רואים היום על פסגות העולם... התחיל פעם אחת, על הר אחד..."
Use when connecting a world-class achievement back to the reader's own starting point.

## 7. What to Avoid

| Rule | Wrong | Correct |
|------|-------|---------|
| No em dashes | `יפה — וגדול` | `יפה וגדול` |
| No en dashes | `2023–2026` | `בין 2023 ל-2026` |
| No vague superlatives | `זה היה מדהים ביותר` | `זה היה הטיפוס הקשה מבין השבעה` |
| No passive where active works | `השיא נשבר` | `קאמי ריטה שבר את השיא` |
| No generic openings | `בכתבה הזו נסקור את...` | Start with the story itself |
| No clickbait headlines | `10 עובדות שלא ידעתם!` | Descriptive, honest title |
| No exclamation marks in body | `הוא עלה 32 פעמים!` | `הוא עלה לפסגה 32 פעמים.` |

## 8. Post Metadata Checklist

Every post object in `blogData.js` needs all of these fields:

```js
{
  id:              // unique integer, increment from last
  slug:            // URL-safe, e.g. 'kami-rita-32-everest-summit-2026'
  title:           // Hebrew title
  titleEn:         // English title
  author:          'HighAir Expeditions'
  dateIso:         // 'YYYY-MM-DD'
  dateModified:    // 'YYYY-MM-DD' (same as dateIso on first publish)
  dateHe:          // 'DD בחודש YYYY'
  dateEn:          // 'Month DD, YYYY'
  category:        // Hebrew: 'חדשות' or 'מדריכים'
  categoryEn:      // English: 'News' or 'Guides'
  img:             // '/images/blog/filename.webp'
  imgPosition:     // e.g. 'center 20%'
  imgPositionCard: // same or slightly adjusted
  imgCredit:       // '' if none, or 'Photographer name'
  excerpt:         // Hebrew, 1-2 sentences, punchy
  excerptEn:       // English version of excerpt
  content:         // Hebrew content array
  contentEn:       // English content array
}
```

Place the new post at the **top** of the `POSTS` array (newest first).

## 9. Block Type Reference

| Type | Purpose | Required fields |
|------|---------|----------------|
| `text` | Body paragraph | `value` |
| `section` | Major section header | `value` |
| `heading` | FAQ question (or sub-question inside section) | `value` |
| `subheading` | Sub-section header within a section | `value` |
| `callout` | Highlighted fact box | `title`, `value` |
| `image` | Inline image or placeholder | `src` or `grad`, optional `alt`, `caption` |
| `list` | Bulleted list | `items: []` |
| `cta` | Call-to-action button | `text`, `textEn`, `href` |

## 10. SEO Notes

- The slug goes directly into the URL. Keep it descriptive, lowercase, hyphens only.
- The sitemap is auto-generated at build time from the slugs in `blogData.js`. No manual update needed.
- HTML shells are also pre-rendered automatically. Run `npm run build` after adding a post.
- `{ type: 'section', value: 'שאלות נפוצות' }` triggers automatic FAQPage JSON-LD schema. Do not rename this section.
- The `excerpt` field becomes the meta description. Keep it under 160 characters.
- Title: aim for 50-60 characters, include the key person or concept name.
